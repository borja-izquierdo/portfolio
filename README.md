## Featured research

**[Forecasting and trading the S&P 500 variance risk premium](https://github.com/borja-izquierdo/vrp-sp500-options)**
MSc thesis, revised and extended. Gradient boosting with purged walk-forward cross-validation forecasts the variance risk premium; a delta-hedged SPX straddle backtest, synthetic variance swaps and bootstrap inference test whether the forecast adds trading value.
[Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/borja-izquierdo/vrp-sp500-options/blob/main/paper/en/vrp_en.pdf) · Python, XGBoost, LightGBM, LaTeX
